BOXUP LOCAL TESTING STEPS

1. Open XAMPP Control Panel.
2. Start Apache.
3. Put this whole Boxup-updated folder inside:
   C:\xampp\htdocs
4. Rename the folder to:
   boxup
5. Open this URL in your browser:
   http://localhost/boxup/
6. Test the contact form on:
   http://localhost/boxup/contact.html
7. Test the newsletter form in the footer.
8. Check saved submissions inside:
   C:\xampp\htdocs\boxup\storage

Expected files after testing:

- contact-submissions.jsonl
- newsletter-signups.jsonl
- rate-contact-...json
- rate-newsletter-...json

Notes:

- Opening index.html directly is fine for frontend viewing.
- Backend forms require Apache/PHP and must be opened through http://localhost/boxup/.
- PHP is available through XAMPP at:
  C:\xampp\php\php.exe
